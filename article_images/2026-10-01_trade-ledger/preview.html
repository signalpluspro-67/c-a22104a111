The scoreboard

The stops are small by design. Each one sits under the level that made the trade, so a wrong idea costs a few percent, and one target pays for several of them.

How we count

  • Entry at the level written in the letter, never at a better opening price.
  • If the stop and a target fall on the same day, the stop counts.
  • A target range counts at its low end. The result is the highest target reached before the stop.
  • Ideas that never reached their entry are left out: $ELV, $GOOGL.
  • Open ideas are marked to the last close and stay on the list until they close.
