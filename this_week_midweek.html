The Rotation Is Knocking While Nobody Answers

Wednesday, November 18 2026

The Saturday thesis is still on track, but the tape got quieter than I expected. Two sessions in and nothing has broken. What we called a market running on three cylinders is now running on two and a half, and you can see it in the numbers.

Let me show you what changed since Saturday.

The broad market stalled. SPY closed at 729.84, down 0.4% on the day. QQQ finished at 656.78, flat to the decimal. That is the part of the market that carries the index, and it is tired. The Magnificent crowd that dragged everything higher through October is now chopping sideways, and chopping sideways at these levels is a tell. When the leaders stop rising, they are distributing shares to you. I am not calling a top. I am telling you the fuel is thinning out.

Then look at what did rise. IWM at 270.57, up 0.6%, the only green in the major tape. Small caps outperformed the S&P by a full point on a down day. That is the rotation the Saturday piece flagged, and it is still knocking. The problem is nobody answered the door. Volume in IWM stayed light. Breadth stayed narrow. You get a bid under small caps without a real expansion in participation, and that is a rotation that can stall on the next bad headline.

The fear gauge confirms the same story. VIX at 25.12, down 1.3%. Under 20 is calm, above 30 is panic, and 25 is the wary middle. The market is not comfortable, and it is not scared. It is waiting. Ranges this tight usually resolve in a direction, and the direction tends to favor whoever is patient enough to be positioned ahead of it.

Our desk setups tell you where I am leaning. IBP at $236.73 has all three confluence tags: volume leader, bottom, pullback. LULU at $111.60 carries the same three flags. KSPI at $89.15 shows the moving average cross on top. Three long setups, all flagged for pullback entries. Translation: we are buying dips into levels, not chasing breakouts. That is the posture a 25 VIX demands.

Here is the philosophy. A rotation you can see but cannot confirm is a rotation you size small and respect. I do not need the small cap bid to be right today. I need it to hold the recent low if the mega caps wobble. If SPY breaks under 725, the small cap bid is going to get tested hard, and I want to know that before I add risk.

What I am watching into Friday.

First, IWM's 270 handle. Held, and the rotation is alive. Lost, and I cut my small cap exposure and let the setups come to me.

Second, VIX 27, the top of this week's range. Break that and the market is telling you the choppy tape is going to get violent. I would rather be wrong standing aside than right holding full size.
