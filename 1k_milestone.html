What makes a Note travel

Writers restack, readers just like. Reach on Substack comes from other writers hitting restack, so a Note has to hand them one usable idea about the craft. "I published less and grew faster" is that idea. A pure thank-you gets hearts from people who already subscribe and goes nowhere new.

Short beats complete. Viral Notes are 3 to 6 lines. One hook, one payload, stop. Every extra sentence is a reason to scroll past. All eight below are cut to the bone on purpose.

Selling kills the share. Nobody restacks a promo. Keep the Note clean, keep the ask in the email P.S.

The sequence

  1. Day 1, morning — Post the viral Note. Nothing else that day.
  2. Day 1, first 2 hours — Reply to every comment. That window decides the reach.
  3. Day 2 or 3 — Send the celebration email. Body celebrates, P.S. carries the ask.
  4. Day 5 — Second Note, different angle, only if the first one moved.
  5. Hold — No discount. Save that lever for 3K.

The P.S. problem

The trial is already permanent, so "unlocked for the milestone" is a lie your readers can check. Anyone who has seen the paywall once has seen the button. Framing an everyday offer as a celebration gift burns the trust the rest of the email just built.

Give a real gift instead: unlock a paid post. Costs nothing, isn't available every day, and fixes the actual problem. At 10 paid out of 1,000, nobody is refusing because $200 is too much. They're refusing because they've never seen the output. One real deep dive converts far better than a paragraph describing the deep dive.
