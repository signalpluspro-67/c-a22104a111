Introducing CBZ

CBIZ, Inc. provides financial, insurance, and advisory services to small and medium-sized businesses across the United States and Canada. It runs three segments: Financial Services, Benefits and Insurance Services, and National Practices.

The Financial Services segment handles accounting and tax, financial advisory, and government healthcare consulting. Benefits and Insurance covers employee benefits, payroll, human capital, property and casualty insurance, and retirement services. National Practices provides IT managed networking and hardware services.

The model is sticky. Small businesses outsource these functions because they can't afford full in-house teams. CBIZ collects recurring fees for compliance, benefits administration, and tax work. That's durable revenue, though it is sensitive to the small-business cycle.
