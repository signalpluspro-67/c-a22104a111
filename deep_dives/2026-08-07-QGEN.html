Introducing QGEN

Qiagen N.V. builds sample-to-insight tools for molecular diagnostics and life sciences research.

The business spans several segments. Sample technology includes nucleic acid stabilization and purification kits. The diagnostics pipeline covers immune response testing, oncology, sexual and reproductive health, and prenatal testing. The research side offers PCR consumables, digital PCR and qPCR instruments, NGS gene panels, library prep kits, and bioinformatics software. Qiagen also provides software-as-a-service and forensic genetic genealogy services.

The edge is the installed base. Once a lab buys a Qiagen instrument, the consumables and reagents create a recurring revenue stream that is sticky and high-margin. That is the core of the business model. Gross margin runs at 66.1%, which tells you the consumable pull-through is doing its job. The company serves molecular diagnostics and life sciences sectors globally.
