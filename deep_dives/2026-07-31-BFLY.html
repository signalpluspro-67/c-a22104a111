Introducing BFLY

Butterfly Network makes a single handheld ultrasound probe that replaces an entire cart-based system. The Butterfly iQ+ and iQ3 devices plug into a smartphone or tablet and perform whole-body imaging. The company sells through direct sales, distributors, and e-commerce. It serves hospitals, clinics, and veterinary practices.

The edge is the silicon. Butterfly uses a chip-based ultrasound-on-a-chip transducer instead of the piezoelectric crystals that traditional manufacturers use. That design choice lowers manufacturing cost and enables a single probe to cover the full body. Competitors like GE and Philips still sell multiple probes for different applications. Butterfly sells one.

The software layer is where the company is building its moat. Compass AI connects every device across a hospital system. ScanLab provides AI-powered educational walkthroughs for real-time scanning guidance. Butterfly Move merges handheld mobility with cart stability. The strategy is to own the workflow, not just the hardware. Revenue grew 39.5% over the trailing twelve months, which tells you the market is responding.
