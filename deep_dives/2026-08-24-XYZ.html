Introducing XYZ

Block, Inc. is two large businesses under one roof. Square provides payment hardware, software, and banking services to merchants, from coffee shops to multi-location retailers. Cash App is a consumer financial platform offering peer-to-peer payments, a debit card, direct deposit, stock and bitcoin trading, and tax preparation. The company also runs Afterpay, a buy now, pay later platform, and TIDAL, a music streaming service.

The edge is the ecosystem. A merchant using Square for payments is also likely to use Square for payroll, loyalty, and loans. A consumer using Cash App for splitting a dinner bill is a few taps away from using it for direct deposit, investing, or filing taxes. It is a real business with real cash flows, even if the bottom line is still thin.
