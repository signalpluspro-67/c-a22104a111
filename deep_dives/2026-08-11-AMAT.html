Introducing AMAT

Applied Materials is the largest semiconductor equipment company in the United States by market cap. It designs, manufactures, and services the machines that fabricate integrated circuits. Its customers include every major foundry and memory maker across Taiwan, Korea, China, Japan, and the United States.

The company operates through two segments. Semiconductor Systems is the core business, delivering capital equipment for deposition, etch, chemical mechanical planarization, metrology, ion implantation, and wafer packaging. Applied Global Services provides spares, upgrades, factory automation software, and services that keep those machines running at peak utilization. The services segment creates a recurring revenue base that smooths the cyclical swings inherent in capex spending.

Applied Materials holds an edge in materials engineering specifically. As transistors shrink below 3 nanometers, the engineering challenge shifts from lithography to the materials stack. New interconnects, new dielectrics, and new gate architectures all require deposition and etch steps that Applied Materials dominates.
