Introducing ANGX

Angel Studios is a media company that produces, distributes, and streams films and series. It came out of a rebrand in 2021 from VidAngel, a filtering and streaming pioneer. The business now operates a direct‑to‑consumer platform where viewers watch original and licensed content. It also sells physical media, apparel, and books, and licenses its content. The company runs on a community‑powered model. Fans invest, vote, and promote projects through the Angel Guild.

That community is the edge. Angels fund production through crowdfunding. They become distributors. The cost structure stays lean because marketing spend is partly crowd‑sourced. This is not a capital‑intensive studio that sinks hundreds of millions into marketing and prays for a theatrical opening. It is a platform that matches supply with built‑in demand.
