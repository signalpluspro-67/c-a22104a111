Introducing ARKO

Arko Corp. operates one of the largest independent convenience store chains in the United States. It is based in Richmond, Virginia.

The Retail segment is what you would expect. It sells fuel, merchandise, prepared foods, beverages, tobacco, candy, and groceries directly to consumers. The Wholesale segment supplies fuel to dealers and sub-wholesalers. Fleet Fueling operates cardlock locations and sells fuel through proprietary fuel cards. GPMP handles wholesale fuel distribution to the other segments.

The edge here is scale and vertical integration. Arko controls fuel distribution from the wholesale level through to the retail pump. That structure lets the company capture margin at multiple points in the fuel supply chain.
