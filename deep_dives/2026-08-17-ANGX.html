Introducing ANGX

Angel Studios is a media company built on a crowdfunding model. It produces and distributes films, series, and licensed content through its own streaming platform. The company lets its community vote on what gets made. Users buy in early, fund projects, and share in the upside.

It also sells merchandise, runs theatrical releases, and licenses content. The business originated as VidAngel, a filtering service, before pivoting to original production in 2021. Its most unusual asset is the audience itself: a built-in distribution channel of committed viewers who have a financial and emotional stake in the content’s success. That loyalty shows up in the revenue line.

Risk Factors

The bear case is straightforward. Revenue growth could slow. The balance sheet has negative book value and a current ratio below 1.0. A capital raise would punish the stock.
