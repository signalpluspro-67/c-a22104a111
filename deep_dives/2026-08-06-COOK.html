Introducing COOK

Traeger, Inc. built the wood pellet grill category. The company designs, sources, and sells wood pellet fueled barbecue grills, flat top grills, and a full ecosystem of accessories, wood pellets, rubs, sauces, and branded merchandise. Headquartered in Salt Lake City, Utah, Traeger was founded in 1987 and has roughly 433 employees.

The edge here is the installed base and the consumable revenue model. Every Traeger grill is an internet of things device connected through the Traeger app. That app delivers recipes, instructional videos, and branded lifestyle content from what the company calls the Traegerhood. The grill is the razor. The wood pellets, spices, sauces, replacement parts, and accessories are the blades. Owners keep coming back for pellets and accessories, and that recurring revenue stream is what separates Traeger from a one time appliance sale.

The company sells through retailers, distributors, and direct to consumer channels in the United States and internationally. That omnichannel footprint gives Traeger reach into big box retail while maintaining a direct relationship with the end customer through its app and content library.
