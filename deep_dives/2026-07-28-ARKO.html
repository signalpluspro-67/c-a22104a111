Introducing ARKO

Arko Corp. operates one of the largest independent convenience store chains in the United States. The company runs through four segments: Retail, Wholesale, Fleet Fueling, and GPMP. The Retail segment is what you recognize. It is the stores selling fuel, merchandise, prepared foods, beverages, tobacco, candy, snacks, grocery, and beer directly to consumers.

The Wholesale segment supplies fuel to dealers, sub-wholesalers, and bulk and spot purchasers. Fleet Fueling operates proprietary and third-party cardlock locations and sells fuel through proprietary fuel cards. GPMP handles wholesale distribution of fuel to the retail and wholesale segments. This is a fuel logistics business wrapped around a retail convenience store operator.

Arko has grown through acquisition, snapping up independent convenience store operators and fuel distributors to build scale. The thesis is that scale in convenience stores drives purchasing power on merchandise and fuel, which compounds over time. The challenge is that fuel is a low-margin business and the balance sheet carries the weight of that acquisition strategy.
