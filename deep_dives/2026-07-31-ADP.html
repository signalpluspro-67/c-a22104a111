Introducing ADP

Automatic Data Processing is the largest payroll processor in the United States and one of the oldest technology companies still compounding at scale. Its core franchise is sticky: once a company plugs into ADP for payroll, switching costs are enormous. Rip-and-replace is painful, disruptive, and risky. So clients stay.

The company runs two segments. Employer Services delivers cloud-based HCM platforms including RUN Powered by ADP for small businesses, ADP Workforce Now for mid-market clients, and ADP Lyric HCM for large enterprises. The PEO segment, under the ADP TotalSource brand, offers full HR outsourcing through a co-employment model. That gives ADP a embedded revenue stream from small businesses that want to outsource HR without building internal teams.

The edge is scale and trust. ADP processes payroll for roughly one in six US workers. That data flow gives the company a real-time read on employment trends before the government reports them. Nobody else has that dataset at that depth. It is a moat built on decades of client retention and incremental platform upgrades.
