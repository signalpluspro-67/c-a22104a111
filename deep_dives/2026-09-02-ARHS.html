Introducing ARHS

Arhaus is a premium home furnishings retailer based in Boston Heights, Ohio. Founded in 1986, it sells sofas, dining tables, beds, outdoor furniture, lighting, rugs, and décor through showrooms, ecommerce, catalogs, and interior designer trade services.

The company runs an omni-channel model. That means customers can buy the same product in a showroom, online, or through a designer. The brand leans high-end, yet the stock trades at a discount to its own history. Enterprise value is $1.63 billion. This is a small cap with a real business, real revenue, and real earnings.

What gives it an edge is the premium positioning with a relatively low price-to-sales ratio. You are buying a premium retailer at a 0.9 times sales valuation. That is the kind of mismatch that closes when sentiment shifts.
