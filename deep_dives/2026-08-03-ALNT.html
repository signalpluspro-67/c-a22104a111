Introducing ALNT

Allient Inc. designs and manufactures precision controlled-motion components and systems. The product line spans servo motors, brushless motors, gear motors, optical encoders, servo amplifiers, motion controllers, and nano-precision positioning systems. Headquartered in Amherst, New York, the company was founded in 1939 and renamed from Allied Motion Technologies to Allient in August 2023.

The company serves four end markets: industrial, vehicle, medical, and aerospace and defense. It sells through direct sales, manufacturer representatives, and distributors across the United States, Canada, South America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific.

The edge is engineering depth. Allient makes the components that make machines move with precision. That is a specialized niche with high switching costs. Once a medical device manufacturer or an aerospace contractor designs Allient motors and encoders into a platform, replacing them requires re-certification. That stickiness compounds across multi-year product cycles.
