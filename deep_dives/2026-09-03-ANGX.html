Introducing ANGX

Angel Studios is a media and technology company. It produces and distributes films and series. It also licenses films and shows from other creators. The business runs on a streaming platform where viewers watch movies, shows, and documentaries.

It makes money beyond streaming, too. Merchandise, apparel, DVDs, Blu-rays, books, and a direct online store all feed the top line. There is also theatrical distribution, ticket presales, and a membership program called Angel Guild. A pay-it-forward feature lets one buyer fund another person's ticket, which is a different angle in entertainment.

The company was founded in 2013 as VidAngel and renamed Angel Studios in March 2021. It is based in Provo, Utah. It employs 290 people. That small size is the core of both the opportunity and the risk.
