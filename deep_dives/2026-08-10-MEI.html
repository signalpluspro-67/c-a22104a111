Introducing MEI

Methode Electronics is a 1946 company headquartered in Southfield, Michigan. It designs, engineers, and manufactures mechatronic products across four segments: Automotive, Industrial, Interface, and Medical.

The Automotive segment is the largest revenue driver. It supplies power distribution solutions like busbars, smart connect systems, battery disconnect units, and integrated circuit boards to OEMs and tiered suppliers. This is where EV content per vehicle lifts the growth profile. The Industrial segment makes lighting solutions, safety radio remote controls, flexible cables, and high-current busbars for aerospace, data centers, telecommunications, and transportation.

The Interface segment provides high-speed digital communication over copper media for data networking and broadband markets. That segment ties Methode directly to data-center buildout. The company occupies a niche between commodity component makers and specialized systems providers. Its edge is engineering depth in power distribution and signal integrity, which are both critical as data centers scale up power density and automotive platforms add electrical content.
