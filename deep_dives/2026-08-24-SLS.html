Introducing SLS

SELLAS Life Sciences Group is a late-stage clinical biopharmaceutical company. It develops novel cancer therapies. The lead candidate is galinpepimut-S, or GPS, a peptide immunotherapy targeting the Wilms tumor 1 antigen. It also has SLS009, a selective small molecule CDK9 inhibitor. Both are in clinical trials for hematologic malignancies and solid tumors.

The company works with Merck on a Phase 1/2 trial combining GPS with pembrolizumab. It has a collaboration with GenFleet Therapeutics for GFH009. It also partners with Memorial Sloan Kettering on WT1 peptide vaccine technology. This is a pipeline play. No revenue, no profit, no product sales yet. The entire value rests on clinical progress and the market's willingness to price that progress.
