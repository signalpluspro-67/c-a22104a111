Introducing AMN

AMN Healthcare Services is the largest healthcare staffing company in the United States. It operates through three segments: Nurse and Allied Solutions, Physician and Leadership Solutions, and Technology and Workforce Solutions. The core business places travel nurses, allied health professionals, and locum tenens physicians into hospitals and care facilities that need flexible labor.

The company was founded in 1985 and is based in Dallas, Texas. Its brand portfolio includes AMN Healthcare, Nursefinders, O'Grady Peyton International, Connetics, Medical Search International, DRW Healthcare Staffing, and B.E. Smith.

The edge is scale and technology. AMN pairs its staffing network with vendor management systems and workforce optimization tools. That technology layer lets hospitals manage contingent labor through a single platform. It also gives AMN data on pricing, availability, and demand that smaller competitors lack. When the cycle turns, AMN captures share because hospitals consolidate spend toward the largest provider.
