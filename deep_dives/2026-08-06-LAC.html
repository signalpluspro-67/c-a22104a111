Introducing LAC

Lithium Americas Corp. is a pre-production resource company developing the Thacker Pass lithium project in Humboldt County, northern Nevada. It generates zero revenue. It burns cash. Its entire value rests on bringing Thacker Pass into production.

Thacker Pass sits in the McDermitt Caldera, one of the largest known lithium deposits in the United States. The project has secured a federal loan and permits, which separates it from the pack of junior lithium hopefuls who still face regulatory uncertainty. The company also holds exploration properties in the US and Canada, but Thacker Pass is the only asset that matters for the stock.

Lithium Americas was incorporated in 2023 and is headquartered in Vancouver, Canada. The spinout from the original Lithium Americas entity gave shareholders direct exposure to the US asset. The thesis is simple. If Thacker Pass moves toward production and lithium prices recover, the stock re-rates hard. If neither happens, the stock drifts lower or stays flat. You are buying a binary outcome.
