Introducing ENR

Armor All, STP, and A/C PRO cover the automotive side. The company sells through every channel you can imagine. The portfolio is a mix of high recognition brands in stable categories. Batteries are a replacement business. Auto care is a maintenance business. Neither is glamorous. Both generate repeat revenue.

The edge here is brand equity in categories with high switching costs for retailers. Shelf space at Walmart and Costco is hard to win and hard to lose. Energizer owns that shelf space. The Varta acquisition expanded the international footprint. The auto care portfolio diversifies the revenue base beyond batteries. It is a low growth business. It is a durable one.
