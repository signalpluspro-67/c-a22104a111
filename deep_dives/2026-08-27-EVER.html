Introducing EVER

EverQuote operates an online marketplace for insurance shopping in the United States. The company connects consumers searching for insurance with carriers and agents who want to buy that traffic. It covers automotive, home, and renters insurance.

The business model is simple. A consumer wants insurance quotes. EverQuote captures that demand. It sells the lead to an insurance carrier or agent. The company also provides campaign management tools to its carrier and agent customers.

The edge is data and scale. EverQuote sees massive volumes of consumer intent across insurance categories. That lets it optimize pricing and routing better than a small lead broker. The company is based in Cambridge, Massachusetts, and was founded in 2008.
