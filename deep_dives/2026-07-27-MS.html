Introducing MS

Morgan Stanley is a global financial holding company with three operating segments. Institutional Securities handles capital raising, M&A advisory, equity and fixed income sales, and prime brokerage. Wealth Management runs the advisor-led brokerage and investment advisory business. Investment Management delivers equity, fixed income, alternatives, and liquidity products to institutions and individuals.

The company has spent years shifting its earnings mix toward recurring, fee-based revenue. Wealth Management and Investment Management now carry a larger share of the franchise. That reduces dependence on deal flow and trading volume, which historically swung wildly quarter to quarter.

The Institutional segment still drives the upside when capital markets are open and IPO activity is healthy. You get the cyclical torque from underwriting and advisory on top of a stickier asset-management base.
