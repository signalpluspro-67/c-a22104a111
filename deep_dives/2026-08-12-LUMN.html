Introducing LUMN

Lumen Technologies is a networking company headquartered in Monroe, Louisiana. It was formerly known as CenturyLink and changed its name in September 2020. The company employs 24,000 people and operates in two segments: Business and Mass Markets.

The Business segment sells dark fiber, conduit, edge cloud, IP, voice over IP, managed security, software-defined wide area networks, and optical services. It also sells ethernet and VPN data networks. The Mass Markets segment provides broadband service to residential and small business customers under the Quantum Fiber brand.

Lumen owns the physical infrastructure that connects data centers. Dark fiber and conduit are the raw inputs for AI data center interconnection. The company carries heavy legacy debt from its CenturyLink acquisition history. That debt load is the reason the equity trades where it does.
