The Big Picture

The industrials sector is exhausted. The macro read flags the group with a state of EXHAUSTED and a sector acceleration reading of NEW_DOWN, which means the trend is rolling from neutral into negative territory.

You are looking at a cyclical group that led the market for quarters and is now handing that leadership to defensive sectors. When industrials crack, they crack slowly. The momentum drains first. Then breadth thins. Then the heavyweight names start leaking. That is the stage you are in right now.

This backdrop rewards a patient short seller. You do not need a crash. You need a slow grind lower as the cycle turns and the hot money rotates out. A name like Caterpillar, the largest heavy machinery maker in the world, is exactly where you look for that kind of fade.

Introducing CAT

Caterpillar is the global benchmark for construction and mining equipment. The company builds track-type tractors, wheel loaders, excavators, mining trucks, and off-highway diesel engines through three core segments: Construction Industries, Resource Industries, and Energy & Transportation. A fourth segment, Financial Products, provides dealer and customer financing.

The edge is scale and dealer network density. The service and parts business is the real cash machine. Once a machine is sold, the customer is locked into a decades-long parts and service relationship. That aftermarket stream smooths out the cyclicality.

The vulnerability is the cyclicality itself. Construction and mining capex are late-cycle. When orders slow, the backlog burns down and the operating leverage that amplified profits on the way up works in reverse. You are betting that the cycle has peaked.
