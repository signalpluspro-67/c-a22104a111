Introducing GENI

Genius Sports Limited provides data, streaming, and integrity products to sports leagues, sportsbooks, and media companies. The company runs live optical tracking and event data collection. It sells streaming infrastructure so leagues can distribute their own video. It builds integrity monitoring tools that flag suspicious betting patterns.

Genius also makes BetVision, an interactive single-screen streaming product with integrated bet slips and live stats. It operates FanHub, an advertising platform for data-driven creative and programmatic buying. The company was founded in 2001 and is headquartered in London.

Its edge is the official data relationships with leagues. Sportsbooks need that data feed to price bets. Media companies need the video layer. Leagues need the integrity layer. Genius sits in the middle and gets paid from multiple directions.
