The Big Picture

The lithium trade has been left for dead. Prices for the metal collapsed from their 2022 peaks, and most investors stopped paying attention. That is exactly when a lottery ticket gets interesting.

The structural story has not gone away. The United States wants a domestic lithium supply chain, and it wants one badly. Washington has made clear that reliance on foreign processing is a national security problem. Tax credits, grants, and loan guarantees are flowing toward companies that can build lithium production on American soil.

Basic Materials as a sector sits out of favor. Capital has rotated toward artificial intelligence and software. The money that once chased battery metal equities has moved on. You are looking at a sector with damaged sentiment and a handful of companies still standing.

This backdrop rewards a name with a real asset, government backing, and a stock price that has already been crushed. The downside has been largely spent. The upside, if lithium sentiment turns, is asymmetrical.

Introducing LAC

Lithium Americas Corp. is a development-stage resource company. It does not produce lithium yet. Its flagship asset is the Thacker Pass project in Humboldt County, northern Nevada, located in the McDermitt Caldera, one of the largest known lithium deposits in the United States. The company was incorporated in 2023 and is headquartered in Vancouver, Canada, with 94 employees.

Thacker Pass is the whole ballgame. The project has received federal support, and the company is working toward building both the mine and a chemical processing facility. This is a pre-revenue company. Every dollar of value comes from what Thacker Pass could eventually produce and whether the company can finance getting there.

The edge here is strategic. A domestically sourced lithium project in Nevada with government backing is rare. If the United States gets serious about securing battery materials, Thacker Pass is one of the few assets that matters. The question is whether LAC can survive long enough to see it through.
