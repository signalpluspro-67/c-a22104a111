Introducing ACVA

ACV Auctions runs a digital wholesale marketplace for used vehicles. Selling dealerships list inventory. Buying dealerships bid in real time. The platform layers on condition reports, transportation, short-term inventory financing, and data services that dealers use to price retail units.

It competes with physical auctions and a few other digital entrants. Its edge is the inspection. ACV sends inspectors to the vehicle before it lists. A True360 report becomes the shared language between a seller in Ohio and a buyer in Florida. That trust asset scales with volume because every transaction feeds the pricing data that dealers then license.

It operates at a net loss but generates positive operating cash flow. The balance sheet carries manageable debt. The forward earnings picture turns positive, with analysts pointing to $0.34 in forward EPS. The valuation is 1.6x sales and 22.2x forward earnings, a level that prices in some execution risk but not a premium.
