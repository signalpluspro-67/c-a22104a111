Introducing BROS

Dutch Bros runs a drive-thru coffee chain across the United States. The company operates through company-owned shops and a franchising segment. It sells coffee, its Rebel energy drink line, and related beverages. Founded in 1992 and based in Tempe, Arizona, the company has grown to roughly 24,000 employees.

The edge is speed and volume. Dutch Bros shops are built for high throughput at the drive-thru window. The menu is simple. The staff is young and energetic. The brand has a cult following in its western U.S. stronghold and is expanding eastward. Unit growth is the primary engine here. New shops open, comp sales grow, and the flywheel turns.

This is a growth story priced as one. You are paying for the expansion runway and the margin progression that comes with scale. The company is still early in its national footprint. That means the upside is real but the valuation leaves no room for execution slips.
