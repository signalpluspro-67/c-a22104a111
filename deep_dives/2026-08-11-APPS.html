Introducing APPS

Digital Turbine operates a mobile growth platform that connects advertisers, publishers, carriers, and device manufacturers. The company runs two segments. On Device Solutions delivers apps directly to users through partnerships with wireless carriers and OEMs, giving it a preinstalled distribution channel most competitors cannot replicate. The App Growth Platform handles user acquisition, ad monetization, and programmatic advertising for mobile publishers and developers.

The edge sits in that OEM and carrier relationships. Preinstalled app distribution is a structural advantage because it bypasses the app store chokepoint. Device makers and carriers control what ships on the phone, and Digital Turbine has embedded itself into that pipeline.

The company serves clients across the United States, Europe, the Middle East, Africa, Asia Pacific, China, and Latin America. It employs 620 people and is headquartered in Austin, Texas.
