Introducing ADAM

Adamas Trust, Inc. is a mortgage REIT headquartered in New York. It acquires, finances, and manages mortgage-related residential assets across the United States. The company was formerly known as New York Mortgage Trust and changed its name in September 2025.

The business runs through two segments. Investment Portfolio holds a diversified mix of agency and non-agency residential mortgage-backed securities, residential loans, multifamily loans, preferred equity, and joint venture equity. Constructive is the lending arm. It originates business purpose loans for real estate investors, focused on rental and transitional properties, and sells those loans.

As a REIT, Adamas distributes at least 90% of taxable income to shareholders. That structure forces a high payout. The portfolio mix gives you exposure to both the yield from holding mortgage assets and the fee income from originating and selling loans. That dual revenue stream matters when one side of the business gets squeezed.
