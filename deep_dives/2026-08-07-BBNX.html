Introducing BBNX

Beta Bionics is a commercial-stage medical device company headquartered in Irvine, California. The company builds the iLet Bionic Pancreas, an automated insulin delivery system for type 1 diabetes in adults and children ages six and up. The iLet handles dosing decisions that patients normally make manually, adjusting insulin delivery based on continuous glucose data without requiring the patient to count carbohydrates or set basal rates.

The pipeline extends the platform. A patch pump in development would adhere directly to the skin and eliminate tubing. A bihormonal version of the iLet would deliver both insulin and glucagon, closing the loop on both sides of blood-sugar management. The company is also pursuing type 2 diabetes patients who require insulin, expanding the addressable population beyond the type 1 core.

Beta Bionics has a collaboration with Xeris Pharmaceuticals to develop a pump-compatible glucagon formulation for the bihormonal system. It has a separate agreement with Abbott Diabetes Care to integrate the iLet with Abbott's continuous glucose monitoring technology. These partnerships matter because automated insulin delivery depends on reliable glucose data and stable glucagon formulations. The company has roughly 420 employees.
