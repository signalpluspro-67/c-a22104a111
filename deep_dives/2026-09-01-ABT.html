Introducing ABT

Abbott Laboratories makes healthcare products across four segments: Established Pharmaceutical Products, Diagnostic Products, Nutritional Products, and Medical Devices. It was founded in 1888 and is based in Abbott Park, Illinois.

The device segment includes heart devices, diabetes care, and neuromodulation. The diagnostics segment covers lab systems, point-of-care testing, and rapid tests for HIV, influenza, and RSV. Nutrition products include infant formula and adult nutrition. The established pharma business sells a portfolio of generic drugs in emerging markets. That four-part structure means no single product line dominates the P&L.

Abbott's edge is scale and distribution. The dividend yield is 2.24% on a $2.52 annual rate. That income stream supports the stock during sideways stretches.
