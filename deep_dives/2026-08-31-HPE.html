Introducing HPE

Hewlett Packard Enterprise is the enterprise infrastructure side of the old HP split. It builds servers, storage, networking gear, and hybrid cloud software. It also runs a financing arm that helps customers buy all of it flexibly.

Five segments drive the business. Server is the biggest, with ProLiant racks, Synergy integrated systems, Edgeline edge servers, and the Cray supercomputing line. Hybrid Cloud includes GreenLake, the consumption-based offering. Networking sells Aruba, acquisition-heavy switches, routers, and Wi-Fi gear. Financial Services provides leasing and flexible consumption models. Corporate Investments and Other is the leftover bucket.

The edge is distribution and breadth. HPE sells through resellers, distributors, OEMs, integrators, and advisory firms. It reaches commercial, enterprise, and public sector buyers. It is a real, scaled operator, not a story stock.
