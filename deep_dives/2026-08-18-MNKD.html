Introducing MNKD

MannKind Corporation is a biopharma company that figured out how to deliver drugs through the lungs. Its core technology is a dry powder inhalation platform. It commercializes treatments for diabetes, pulmonary hypertension, and the fluid overload that comes with heart failure and chronic kidney disease. The portfolio is already real, not a pipeline dream.

Afrezza is the inhaled insulin you may have heard about. V-Go is a wearable insulin delivery device. Tyvaso DPI treats pulmonary arterial hypertension. FUROSCIX is a furosemide injection for fluid buildup. The pipeline pushes further into orphan lung diseases with a dry powder nintedanib and another early stage asset. The company has a collaboration with United Therapeutics that pays the bills on Tyvaso DPI.

It's not profitable on a GAAP basis yet, but its gross margin tells you the unit economics work. The operating loss is a whisper at -0.8% margin. The negative book value is an accounting artifact of its history, not a verdict on its current business.
