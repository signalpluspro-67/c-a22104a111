Introducing ABCL

AbCellera Biologics is a Canadian antibody discovery company. It builds platforms that find and develop antibody-based medicines for partners. It does not sell a drug yet. It sells the engine that produces drug candidates.

The lead internal program is ABCL635, a non-hormonal medicine in Phase 2 for moderate-to-severe vasomotor symptoms. There is also ABCL575, a Phase 1 monoclonal antibody for T-cell-mediated autoimmune conditions like atopic dermatitis. The real business is the partnership layer: Lilly, AbbVie, Jazz, Biogen, Vertex, and Viking Global all have deals to use its discovery tech for multispecific T-cell engagers and other targets.

Think of ABCL as a royalty and milestone pipeline with a services backbone.
