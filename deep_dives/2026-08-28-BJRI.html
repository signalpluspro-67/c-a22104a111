Introducing BJRI

BJ's Restaurants operates full-service restaurants across the United States. The menu hangs on pizza, beer, appetizers, entrees, wings, pasta, salads, and the Pizookie dessert. It has done this since 1978, first as Chicago Pizza & Brewery, then as BJ's from August 2004. Headquarters sit in Huntington Beach, California.

The model is casual dining with a brewery angle. That gives it a slightly differentiated draw versus generic family dining. You get a full menu plus a craft beer lineup. The combination supports higher average checks and repeat visits.

It is not a rapid unit grower. It is a margin and traffic story inside a mature footprint. The edge is operational, not structural.
