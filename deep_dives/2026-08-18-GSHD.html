Introducing GSHD

Goosehead Insurance is a personal lines insurance agency. It sells homeowner, auto, flood, umbrella, and small business policies. The company operates through two channels. Corporate agents work directly for Goosehead. Franchise agents own their own books and pay Goosehead a royalty. The model is built to scale. A franchisee funds the local office and does the selling. Goosehead provides the brand, the carrier relationships, the technology, and the back office support. It earns a high-margin royalty on every policy that franchisee writes. The corporate channel gives Goosehead full economics in key markets and a laboratory to refine its playbook before franchisees adopt it. The result is a capital-light distribution network with a growing footprint.

The company went public in 2018 and has expanded its agent count steadily. Headquartered in Westlake, Texas, it employs roughly 1600 people. The stock is a small cap with a 1.35 beta, meaning it swings harder than the broad market. That amplifies both gains and drawdowns. The current price is $64.78. A 52-week range that stretches from $33.68 to $90.79 tells you the ride has been anything but smooth. The opportunity today sits in the gap between that $90.79 high and where the stock now trades.
