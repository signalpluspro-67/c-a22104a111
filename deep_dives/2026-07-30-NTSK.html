Introducing NTSK

Netskope is a cybersecurity company based in Santa Clara, California. The company built the Netskope One platform, a cloud-native architecture for securing enterprise digital interactions.

The platform spans three product groups. Security products include cloud access security broker, data loss prevention, threat protection, secure web gateway, private access, and remote browser isolation. Networking products cover Firewall-as-a-Service, software-defined wide-area network, and IoT security. Analytics products deliver user behavior analytics, data lineage, and digital experience management.

The edge is the integrated platform approach. Competitors often sell point solutions that enterprises stitch together. Netskope One handles security, networking, and analytics in a single cloud-native stack. That integration matters as enterprises consolidate vendors and push toward zero-trust frameworks.
