Introducing ANGX

Angel Studios is a media and technology company out of Provo, Utah. It produces and distributes films and series. It runs its own streaming platform where users watch movies, shows, and documentaries. The company also sells apparel, DVDs, Blu-rays, and books directly and through wholesale partners. It licenses content, offers memberships, presells theatrical tickets, and distributes to theaters.

It used to be called VidAngel. It changed its name in March 2021. It operates in the Entertainment industry.

The edge here is the distribution model. Angel Studios built a platform that blends streaming, theatrical, and direct merchandising. It raises capital through its community, which creates a built-in audience before a project hits the screen. That model can compress the cost of content and widen the margin on a hit. It’s not a blockbuster factory. It’s a targeted content engine with a loyal base.
