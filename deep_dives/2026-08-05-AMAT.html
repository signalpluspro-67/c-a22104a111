Introducing AMAT

Applied Materials builds the machines that make chips. The company operates two segments. Semiconductor Systems delivers the capital equipment for etch, deposition, inspection, and packaging. Applied Global Services sells spares, upgrades, and factory automation software to keep those machines running.

The edge is scale. When a fab needs a new deposition tool or an upgrade to an existing line, AMAT is one of three companies on the shortlist.

The services segment adds recurring revenue on top of equipment sales, which smooths the cyclicality that defines this industry.
