Introducing PGEN

Precigen is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company headquartered in Germantown, Maryland. It develops gene and cell therapies using precision technology across immuno-oncology, autoimmune disorders, and infectious diseases.

The company operates two core platforms. UltraCAR-T is its chimeric antigen receptor T cell therapy approach for cancer patients. AdenoVerse uses a library of proprietary adenovectors for gene delivery of therapeutic effectors, immunomodulators, and vaccine antigens. These platforms give Precigen multiple shots on goal across different disease areas.

The pipeline includes several programs in active trials. PRGN-3005 is in a Phase 1/1b trial for advanced ovarian cancer. PRGN-3006 is in a Phase 1b trial for relapsed or refractory acute myeloid leukemia. PRGN-2009 is in a Phase 2 trial for HPV-positive solid tumors. PRGN-2012 is in a Phase 1/2 trial for recurrent respiratory papillomatosis. The company also offers UltraPorator, a proprietary electroporation device for cell engineering. Precigen was formerly known as Intrexon Corporation and rebranded in early 2020.
