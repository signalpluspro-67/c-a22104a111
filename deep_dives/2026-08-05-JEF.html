Introducing JEF

Jefferies Financial Group is a mid-market investment bank and capital-markets firm. It operates in two segments: Investment Banking and Capital Markets, and Asset Management. The banking side provides M&A advisory, debt and equity underwriting, restructuring, and private capital advisory. The trading side covers equities research, sales, and trading, prime brokerage, and fixed-income products across credit, rates, and emerging markets.

The company has carved out a defensible niche. It focuses on middle-market clients that the bulge bracket ignores or under-serves. That positioning wins mandates in a market where deal flow is recovering but large-cap advisory remains concentrated among the top tier.

Jefferies was formerly Leucadia National Corporation, a diversified holding company, before rebranding in 2018. The legacy Leucadia assets have largely been spun off or sold.
