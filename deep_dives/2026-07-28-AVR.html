Introducing AVR

Anteris Technologies Global Corp. is a structural heart company based in Toowong, Australia. The company develops and commercializes minimally invasive medical devices to treat heart valve diseases.

The flagship product is the DurAVR transcatheter heart valve system. It is a novel transcatheter aortic valve designed to treat severe aortic stenosis. The valve is shaped to mimic the performance of a healthy human aortic valve. That design matters because it addresses a core limitation of older transcatheter valves, which do not replicate natural blood flow.

Anteris also develops ADAPT anti-calcification tissue. This technology transforms xenograft tissue into durable bioscaffolds that mimic human tissue for surgical repair. The ComASUR delivery system rounds out the platform, giving physicians a steerable catheter for precise navigation through heart anatomy. Together these products form a coherent platform in a high-growth corner of medical devices.
