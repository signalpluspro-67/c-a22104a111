Introducing VZ

Verizon is the largest domestic wireless carrier by revenue. It operates two segments: Consumer and Business. The Consumer segment sells wireless service under the Verizon and TracFone brands, fixed wireless access broadband, and wireline services through its Fios fiber-optic network in the Mid-Atlantic and Northeast. The Business segment provides wireless and wireline communications, corporate networking, security, managed network services, and IoT products to enterprise and government customers.

The edge is scale and network quality. Verizon owns the premier wireless network in the United States by coverage and reliability metrics. That premium network supports stickier postpaid subscribers and lower churn.

The fixed wireless access product is the growth lever. FWA lets Verizon sell home broadband using its wireless towers instead of digging fiber to every door. It is capital-light and accelerates broadband subscriber growth without the cost of a full fiber build. This is where Verizon is stealing share from cable operators.
