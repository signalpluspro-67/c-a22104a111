Introducing BNED

Barnes & Noble Education runs bookstores on college and university campuses across the United States. The company operates physical stores, virtual bookstores, and e-commerce sites for school spirit apparel. It serves public universities, private colleges, community colleges, trade schools, and online institutions.

The core business is course materials. That includes new and used print textbooks, digital textbooks, and publisher-hosted digital courseware. The company's First Day programs bundle faculty-required materials into a single charge delivered on or before the first day of class at below-market rates. That program is the structural shift you want to be on the right side of. It converts discretionary student spending into institutionally committed revenue.

Beyond course materials, the company sells general merchandise including collegiate apparel, school spirit products, technology products, supplies, and convenience items. It also provides inventory management and point-of-sale software to college bookstores. It was founded in 1965.
