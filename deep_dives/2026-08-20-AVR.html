Introducing AVR

Anteris Technologies Global Corp. makes one thing: a better aortic valve. Its lead product is the DurAVR transcatheter heart valve system, a device designed to mimic the shape and performance of a healthy human aortic valve. Severe aortic stenosis kills people when left untreated. Surgical valve replacement works but carries high risk for elderly patients. Transcatheter valves are the minimally invasive answer, and DurAVR aims to be the next generation.

The company also owns ADAPT anti-calcification tissue technology. This process transforms animal tissue into durable bioscaffolds that resist calcification longer than conventional treatments. A companion ComASUR delivery system gives physicians a steerable, balloon-expandable catheter for precise placement. The investment case rests on future regulatory approvals and adoption.

Analyst consensus is a strong buy with a mean target of $15.33. The company burns cash as it develops its pipeline, but cash per share sits at $2.90 against minimal debt. This is a binary medtech story with a clean balance sheet and a focused product.
