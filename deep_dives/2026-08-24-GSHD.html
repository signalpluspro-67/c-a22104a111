Introducing GSHD

Goosehead Insurance is a personal lines insurance agency built on a franchise model. The company distributes homeowner’s, auto, umbrella, flood, and small business policies through corporate offices and a growing network of franchise locations. It does not underwrite risk. It earns commissions by matching clients with carriers. That is a capital-light, high-margin business.

The edge is the franchise system. Goosehead recruits agents, trains them, and gives them technology to run their own books. Corporate gets a royalty stream and a share of renewals. The model scales without the heavy fixed costs of adding hundreds of W2 employees. This is not a startup experiment. The company was founded in 2003 and is headquartered in Westlake, Texas.
