Introducing AMC

AMC Entertainment Holdings owns, operates, or has interests in movie theaters across the United States and internationally. The company was founded in 1920 and is headquartered in Leawood, Kansas. It runs one of the largest theatrical exhibition networks in the world.

Revenue comes from ticket sales and concessions. The business carries massive fixed costs in rent, labor, and film licensing. That creates high operating margins when attendance is strong and deep losses when it is not. Operating margin on a trailing basis is 14.9%. Gross margin is 18.2%.

The balance sheet is the story. Book value per share is negative 1.63. You are buying a heavily indebted operator with negative equity. The trade is about price movement, not business quality.

The third risk is the setup itself. A 2.70 stock with an average true range of 0.15 requires patience. The stop at 2.18 is 12.1% below entry. The target at 2.85 is 14.9% above.
