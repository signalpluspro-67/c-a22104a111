Introducing ATHM

Autohome Inc. runs the dominant online platform for car buyers in China. Its websites, autohome.com.cn and che168.com, deliver content, tools, and transaction capabilities to consumers and dealers. The company generates revenue through media advertising, dealer subscriptions, lead generation, and an online used car marketplace. It also earns commissions on auto financing and insurance products.

The business is an entrenched intermediary in China's massive auto market. It sits between manufacturers, dealers, and consumers. The brand is well known. The user base is large and sticky. The company has operated since 2004 and is headquartered in Beijing.
