Introducing ACVA

ACV Auctions runs a wholesale digital marketplace for used vehicles between dealers. A selling dealer lists a car. Buying dealers bid in a live auction. The platform handles the inspection, the condition report, the transportation quote, and the financing. ACV takes a cut on each transaction.

The company has built a full stack around that core auction. ACV Transportation gives buyers real-time shipping quotes. ACV Capital offers short-term inventory financing so buyers can purchase vehicles on the platform. Go Green is a customer assurance program covering defect claims. True360 provides cosmetic and structural vehicle assessments integrated into vehicle history reports. ACV MAX is inventory management software that helps dealers price and turn vehicles. The company also runs remarketing centers that handle reconditioning and storage for fleet, rental car, and financial-sector consignors.

That bundle matters. Each layer adds revenue per transaction and increases switching costs. A dealer using ACV for auctions, financing, transportation, and inventory management is expensive to displace. The company employs roughly 3,200 people and is headquartered in Buffalo, New York. It was incorporated in 2014.
