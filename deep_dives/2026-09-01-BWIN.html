Introducing BWIN

The Baldwin Insurance Group, formerly BRP Group, is an independent insurance distribution firm. It runs three segments. Insurance Advisory Solutions handles private risk, commercial risk, employee benefits, and Medicare for businesses and high-net-worth families. Underwriting, Capacity & Technology Solutions houses the MGA platform. Mainstreet Insurance Solutions covers personal, commercial, and life and health products for communities through independent contractor agents.

The company rebranded in May 2024 and sits in Tampa, Florida. It employs 5000 people. The model blends organic distribution with a technology-enabled capacity arm. That dual focus matters. The advisory side produces the relationship revenue. The MGA platform adds product leverage and margin potential.

The edge here is distribution density. Baldwin ties together high-net-worth placement, middle-market commercial, and Mainstreet reach in one platform. Insurance buyers stay sticky. The independent agent network extends the sales force without adding fixed overhead.
