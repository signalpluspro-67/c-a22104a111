Introducing ENR

Energizer Holdings makes and sells household batteries, specialty batteries, automotive care products, and lighting products. You know the brands. Energizer, Eveready, Rayovac, Varta, Armor All, and STP sit on shelves in every major retailer. The company reaches consumers through mass merchandisers, warehouse clubs, drug stores, convenience stores, e-commerce, and military stores.

The business is simple. People buy batteries and car care products repeatedly. The portfolio spans primary batteries, rechargeable options, hearing aid batteries, fuel additives, air conditioning recharge kits, and flashlights. Energizer also licenses its brands for solar products, automotive batteries, portable power, and generators.

Incorporated in 2015 and headquartered in Saint Louis, Missouri, the company employs 6,050 people. It spins off $2.98 billion in trailing revenue from a portfolio of brands most households recognize. The edge is brand familiarity and shelf dominance.
