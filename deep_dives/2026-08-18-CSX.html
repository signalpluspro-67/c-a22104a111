Introducing CSX

CSX Corporation moves freight across the eastern United States and into parts of Canada. The rail segment is the core. It runs 3400 locomotives on roughly 20,000 route miles and connects major population centers to ports, factories, and farms. The trucking segment handles the last mile and intermodal transfers. The mix spans coal, chemicals, autos, forest products, and consumer goods in containers. That breadth means CSX is a proxy for broad industrial and consumer demand, not just one commodity cycle.

It employs 22,200 people and runs approximately 30 intermodal terminals. The network reaches deep water ports for export coal and serves every major market east of the Mississippi. CSX is a simple business. It operates a toll road on rails. Fixed costs are high, margins are wide when volumes run, and cash conversion is strong. You are looking at a legacy physical asset that throws off real free cash flow.
