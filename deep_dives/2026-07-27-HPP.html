Introducing HPP

Hudson Pacific Properties is a Los Angeles-based office REIT incorporated in 1997. It owns and operates office and studio space in global tech and media hubs. The company's pitch is that it serves tenants in converging growth industries, and that its properties are high-barrier, amenitized, and built for collaboration. Think soundstages and Class A office towers in markets where land is scarce and replacement cost is high.

That is the nature of the REIT model. You own real estate, you borrow against it, you lease it out, and you distribute the cash flow.

Hudson Pacific's edge is its niche. It targets tech and media tenants who need specialized space. Studio soundstages are not generic office boxes. They command premium rents and attract long-tenancy leases from content producers who cannot easily relocate. That specialization is the bull case for the operating business. The bear case is that tech and media tenants have been shedding space, and the balance sheet is heavy.
