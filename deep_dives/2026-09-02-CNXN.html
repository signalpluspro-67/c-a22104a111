Introducing CNXN

PC Connection, Inc. sells information technology solutions through three segments: Enterprise Solutions, Business Solutions, and Public Sector Solutions. It offers computer systems, data center gear, artificial intelligence products, managed services, cybersecurity, multicloud, and supply chain services. It also distributes hardware, software, accessories, and cloud solutions through its websites.

The company serves large enterprises, small and medium businesses, healthcare, manufacturing, retail, federal and state government, and education from K-12 through higher ed. The model is simple: source products from manufacturers, add configuration and services, and deliver to customers who need the stuff.

The edge is breadth and service. PC Connection handles design, configuration, and implementation, plus lifecycle and supply chain work. That puts it beyond a simple box mover.
