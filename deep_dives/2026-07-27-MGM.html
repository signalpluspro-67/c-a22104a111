Introducing MGM

MGM Resorts International runs casino resorts across the Las Vegas Strip, regional US markets, Macau, and a growing digital platform. The company has 60,000 employees and a portfolio that includes Bellagio, MGM Grand, and Aria. Its four segments are Las Vegas Strip Resorts, Regional Operations, MGM China, and MGM Digital.

The Las Vegas Strip is the cash engine. Conventions, leisure travel, and premium gaming drive the segment. Regional operations give you geographic diversification across the US. MGM China exposes you to Macau, the largest gaming market in the world. BetMGM is the digital arm, competing in online sports betting and iGaming.

The edge here is scale and location. MGM owns the prime real estate on the Strip. That real estate generates convention business that competitors cannot replicate. The digital business gives you a growth optionality layer on top of a mature physical footprint.
