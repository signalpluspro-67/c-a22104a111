Introducing ADP

Automatic Data Processing runs the plumbing of American employment. It operates two segments. Employer Services sells cloud based HCM platforms like RUN for small business payroll, Workforce Now for mid sized and large firms, and Lyric HCM for analytics and talent. The PEO segment, under ADP TotalSource, bundles HR outsourcing with a co employment model, benefits, and risk management.

The edge is scale and stickiness. Once a company plugs payroll into ADP, the switching cost is high. Miss a pay cycle or a tax filing and the business gets sued. That locks in recurring revenue. The company was founded in 1949. It has been paying people longer than most of its clients have existed.

The forward P/E is 20.9. You are not paying a software multiple for a software business. You are paying a steady cash compounder multiple. Debt to equity is 0.91. The current ratio is thin at 1.05, and the quick ratio is 0.15. That is the one place you pause. More on that later.
