Introducing AOS

A.O. Smith Corporation makes water heaters, boilers, and water treatment products. The company operates in North America, China, Europe, and India. It sells through wholesale plumbing distributors and retail channels. Its brands include A. O. Smith, State, Lochinvar, and Aquasana.

It was founded in 1874 and is headquartered in Milwaukee, Wisconsin. This is a mature, slow-growth industrial. It is not a growth story. It is a replacement cycle business tied to housing and commercial construction.

The edge historically was a high-return capital structure and a dominant market position. Return on equity sits at 28.3%. That number is now shrinking. Earnings fell -10.5% year over year. The moat is intact. The growth is gone.
