Introducing AAL

American Airlines Group is a network air carrier operating out of major hubs across the United States and partner gateways internationally. The company runs a mainline fleet of 1,013 aircraft and serves passengers and cargo through Charlotte, Chicago, Dallas/Fort Worth, Los Angeles, Miami, New York, Philadelphia, Phoenix, and Washington, D.C.

The airline business is a volume game with thin margins and heavy capital requirements. American competes on route coverage, loyalty programs, and operational reliability. Its network reach across the Atlantic, Pacific, and Latin America gives it revenue diversification that smaller carriers lack. It also means exposure to geopolitical and fuel shocks that can compress margins in a hurry.

The balance sheet carries significant leverage from years of fleet investment and pandemic era losses. That is the trade off. This is a turnaround story where the market is pricing in recovery from depressed earnings.
