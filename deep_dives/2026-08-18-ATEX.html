Introducing ATEX

Anterix Inc. is a spectrum landlord. It owns the largest licensed 900 MHz broadband block in the United States. The company sells access to electric, gas, and water utilities that need to overlay their own private LTE networks on top of their grid infrastructure.

Think of it less as a telecom and more as a toll road. The 900 MHz band travels farther through walls and terrain than higher bands. That makes it the backbone for field area networks connecting substations, smart meters, and distributed energy devices. The company has just 63 employees. The asset is the license, the ecosystem is the moat.

Anterix offers a few access models. TowerX provides tower site access. CatalyX delivers a turnkey managed connectivity solution. The Anterix Active Ecosystem bundles third party hardware and software partners that build on the spectrum. The core asset is the same in every deal. Recurring, long term spectrum leases that compound as more utilities join.
