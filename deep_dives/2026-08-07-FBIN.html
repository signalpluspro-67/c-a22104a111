The Big Picture

The industrials sector is neutral and rolling into a fresh downtrend. You have a macro backdrop that punishes anything tied to new construction. The sector acceleration reads NEW_DOWN. That is the wind in your face.

Housing is bifurcated. New builds remain pressured by rates near cycle highs. Repair and remodel activity holds up better because homeowners with 3% or 4% mortgages stay put and fix what they have. That split matters for stock selection. You want the company that sells into repair and remodel, ground-up construction. Pure builders get hurt. The replacement cycle keeps cash registers moving.

This backdrop rewards a name with branded market share, diversified channels, and cash generation that survives a slow volume environment. A company that trades at a compressed forward multiple because the market sees "housing" and lumps it with the builders. That is the setup you are looking at here.

Introducing FBIN

Fortune Brands Innovations sells home, security, and digital products for residential repair, remodeling, new construction, and security applications. The company operates through three segments: Water, Outdoors, and Security. The Water segment covers faucets, accessories, kitchen sinks, and waste disposals under brands like Moen, ROHL, Riobel, and Victoria+Albert. Outdoors manufactures fiberglass and steel entry doors under Therma-Tru, storm and screen doors under Larson, composite decking under Fiberon, and urethane millwork under Fypon. Security offers locks and safety devices under Master Lock, American Lock, Yale, and August, plus fire-resistant safes under SentrySafe.

The edge is brand concentration in categories where contractors and homeowners do not switch. When a plumber reaches for a Moen replacement cartridge, when a builder spec a Therma-Tru door, when a facility manager orders Master Lock padlocks, that purchase is sticky. The company sells through kitchen and bath dealers, pro-oriented wholesalers, industrial distributors, home centers, showrooms, and e-commerce. That channel diversification spreads risk across professional and DIY demand.

Fortune Brands was incorporated in 1988 and is headquartered in Deerfield, Illinois. It employs roughly 10,000 people. The company spun off from its former parent to focus purely on home and security products. You are buying a pure-play on the branded repair and remodel cycle, the part of housing that holds when rates stay high.
