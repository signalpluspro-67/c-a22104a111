Introducing FTRE

Fortrea Holdings Inc. is a contract research organization, or CRO. It runs clinical trials for drug and medical device makers. That means study design, patient recruitment, site monitoring, data management, and regulatory support.

The company split out from a larger parent in 2023 and is based in Durham, North Carolina. It employs about 14,000 people. The business spans early-stage clinical pharmacology through late-stage phase I to IV studies and real-world evidence.

Fortrea also offers consulting. Market access, health economics, outcomes research. The revenue mix is diversified across trial types and customers. This is a service provider, not a drug developer. It earns fees for running trials, regardless of which molecule wins.
