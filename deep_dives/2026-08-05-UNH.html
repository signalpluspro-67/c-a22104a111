Introducing UNH

UnitedHealth Group is the largest health insurer in the United States. The company operates through two main engines: UnitedHealthcare and Optum. UnitedHealthcare handles insurance benefits for employers, individuals, and government programs like Medicare and Medicaid. Optum is the services arm, split into Optum Health, Optum Insight, and Optum Rx.

The Optum segments make this company different from a traditional insurer. Optum Health delivers care directly. Optum Insight sells software, data, and consulting to hospitals and physicians. Optum Rx manages pharmacy benefits and drug purchasing. This vertical integration means UNH captures profit at multiple points in the healthcare supply chain. You insure the patient, manage their pharmacy spend, and provide the care delivery infrastructure.

That structure creates a deep competitive moat. The company employs 390,000 people and serves markets across the United States and internationally. Scale in healthcare insurance is a genuine advantage. It gives UNH pricing power with providers and negotiating power with drug companies. You are looking at a company that touches nearly every part of the healthcare dollar.
