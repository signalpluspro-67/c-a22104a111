Introducing MTDR

Matador Resources is an independent oil and natural gas producer focused entirely on US onshore basins. The company operates two segments: Exploration and Production, and Midstream. The E&P segment is anchored in the Delaware Basin, the western sub-basin of the Permian in Southeast New Mexico and West Texas. Matador also holds acreage in the Haynesville shale and Cotton Valley plays in Northwest Louisiana.

The Midstream segment is the differentiator. Matador runs its own natural gas processing, oil transportation, and produced water gathering and disposal assets. This vertical integration cuts third-party costs and gives the company control over takeaway capacity. It is the kind of structure that keeps margins fatter when basin differentials widen.

With only 483 employees and a market cap near $7.0 billion, Matador is a mid-sized operator. It is not a tiny wildcatter and not an Exxon-sized giant. The size gives it operational focus. The Midstream arm gives it a financial cushion that many pure-play E&P peers lack.
