Introducing TILE

Interface designs and manufactures modular carpet tile and luxury vinyl flooring for commercial interiors. Its products ship into offices, schools, hospitals, airports, retail spaces, and hospitality venues. The company operates under the Interface, FLOR, NORAPLAN, and NORAMENT brands across the United States, Canada, Latin America, Europe, Africa, Asia, and Australia.
The business mixes direct sales to end users with indirect channels through contractors and distributors. Interface also sells installation systems like TacTiles, adhesives, and project management services. That services layer deepens customer relationships and creates recurring touchpoints beyond the initial product sale.
The company competes on design innovation, sustainability credentials, and product breadth across carpet tile and resilient flooring.
