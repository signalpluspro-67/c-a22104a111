Introducing BFLY

Butterfly Network builds handheld ultrasound probes that plug into a smartphone or tablet and perform whole-body imaging. The core product line, Butterfly iQ+, replaced the traditional piezoelectric crystal array with a single silicon chip. That design choice cut manufacturing costs, shrank the form factor to a pocket-sized probe, and made ultrasound accessible in settings where a $200,000 cart system is impractical or unavailable.

The company sells through direct sales, distributors, and e-commerce. Its addressable market extends beyond hospital radiology into emergency medicine, primary care, veterinary practice, and global health. The iQ3 is the third-generation device. Butterfly Move merges the mobility of the handheld with the stability of a full cart. Compass AI connects devices and workflows across departments. ScanLab provides AI-powered educational walkthroughs for training new users.

The edge here is the chip-based architecture. Every competitor in handheld ultrasound still uses conventional transducer technology. Butterfly's patent-protected silicon approach gives it a cost and integration advantage that is difficult to replicate. The company has 220 employees, is headquartered in Burlington, Massachusetts, and was founded in 2011.
