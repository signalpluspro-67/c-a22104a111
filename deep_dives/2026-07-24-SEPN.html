Introducing SEPN

Septerna is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on G protein-coupled receptor (GPCR) targets. GPCRs are a family of cell-surface receptors that are among the most validated drug targets in pharmacology. Septerna's angle is making oral small molecule drugs that hit these receptors, replacing injectable biologics in several disease areas.

The pipeline has three primary programs. The PTH1R program targets hypoparathyroidism with an oral parathyroid hormone receptor agonist. SEP-631 targets chronic spontaneous urticaria and other mast cell diseases as a negative allosteric modulator of MRGPRX2. The TSHR program goes after Graves' disease and thyroid eye disease. Each program addresses a space where oral dosing would be a meaningful shift for patients.

Septerna also has a collaboration and license agreement with Novo Nordisk. That deal covers multiple potential oral small molecule therapies for metabolic diseases. Having Novo Nordisk as a partner on metabolic targets gives Septerna validation from the dominant player in metabolic therapeutics. The company is headquartered in South San Francisco with 130 employees. It went public after incorporating in 2019 and changed its name from GPCR NewCo to Septerna in June 2021.
