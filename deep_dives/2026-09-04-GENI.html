Introducing GENI

Genius Sports Limited runs the plumbing between live sports and the gambling and media machines that monetize them. It collects optical tracking data from thousands of live events, packages it, and delivers it to sportsbooks, leagues, and broadcasters. The company has about 2,000 employees and is headquartered in London.

The core engine is data distribution. Genius captures live event data from leagues, integrates it, and sells feeds to betting operators who need pre-game and in-game odds. A second segment handles streaming and automated production, letting smaller leagues commercialize their video without a broadcast truck. The integrity business monitors suspicious betting patterns for leagues using statistical processes. Then you have FanHub for advertising and BetVision, an interactive single-screen streaming product that overlays live odds and stats onto game broadcasts.

The edge is simple. Genius has long-term deals with major leagues and federations. Those contracts are hard to replicate. The company has spent two decades wiring itself into stadiums, arenas, and betting platforms. A new entrant can't just show up and offer the same feed quality on the same day.
