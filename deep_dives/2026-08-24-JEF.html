Introducing JEF

Jefferies Financial Group runs two main engines. The Investment Banking and Capital Markets segment advises on mergers, restructurings, and capital raises. It also makes markets in equities, fixed income, and loans. The Asset Management segment houses a collection of alternative investment platforms. The company operates across the Americas, Europe, the Middle East, and Asia Pacific.

The firm competed for decades as a scrappy mid tier player before absorbing its parent Leucadia National in 2018. Today it is a pure play investment bank with a reputation for winning advisory mandates in the middle market and grabbing trading share from bulging bracket banks.

JEF is not a sleepy utility. Earnings swung from a trough to a 159.8% growth rate over the past year. That cyclicality cuts both ways. When deal pipelines freeze, revenue drops fast.
