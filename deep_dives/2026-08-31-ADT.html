Introducing ADT

ADT Inc. provides security, smart home, and interactive monitoring services across the United States. The company sells burglar and life safety alarms, smart cameras, home automation, video surveillance, and personal emergency response systems. Customers sign up for equipment plus a monthly monitoring subscription, and that subscription revenue is the heart of the business.

The company operates through two main classes of customers: residential and small business. Its channels include direct sales, field offices, independent dealers, retail, e-commerce, and lead generation partners. It also runs monitoring centers and field service operations. The installed base is nationwide, and the brand is one of the oldest in American security, founded in 1874.

What gives ADT its edge is scale in monitoring and a brand that homeowners recognize. The company has over 12,200 employees and generated trailing revenue of $5.16 billion.
