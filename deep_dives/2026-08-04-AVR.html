Introducing AVR

Anteris Technologies Global Corp. is a structural heart company based in Toowong, Australia. The company develops minimally invasive medical devices to treat heart valve diseases. Its lead product is the DurAVR transcatheter heart valve system, a device designed to treat severe aortic stenosis. The valve is shaped to mimic the performance of a healthy human aortic valve.

Anteris also develops ADAPT anti-calcification tissue. This technology transforms animal tissue into durable bioscaffolds that resist calcification and mimic human tissue for surgical repair. The ComASUR delivery system is a physician-developed balloon expandable catheter designed for precise navigation through heart anatomy without damaging the aorta. The full platform targets aortic valve replacement with a minimally invasive approach.

This is an early commercial stage company with 174 employees. The pipeline is concentrated in the DurAVR platform. That means every headline about clinical results or regulatory progress moves the stock sharply. You are buying a binary story dressed in a technical setup.
