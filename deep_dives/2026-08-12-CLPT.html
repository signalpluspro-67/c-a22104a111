Introducing CLPT

ClearPoint Neuro builds platforms for minimally invasive brain surgery. It is a commercial-stage medical device company, meaning it has products on the market and revenue coming in, not a pre-revenue biotech burning cash on trials.

The core product line is the ClearPoint system, which combines hardware, software, and disposables into an integrated platform for surgical planning, navigation, and monitoring. The hardware includes a head fixation frame, a computer workstation, and an in-room monitor. The disposables side includes the SmartFrame trajectory device, a hand controller, and the SmartFlow Neuro cannula. The software guides the physician through planning, alignment, navigation to the target, and live procedure monitoring.

ClearPoint also offers the Prism Neuro laser therapy system for tissue ablation, and the IRRAflow system, which is an active fluid exchange platform. Beyond its own products, the company provides consulting services to pharmaceutical and medical technology partners. Those services include benchtop testing, preclinical studies, clinical trial support, and regulatory consultation. The company has collaboration agreements with Philips, UCB Biopharma, Clinical Laserthermia Systems, UCSF, and NE Scientific. That partner roster matters because it means ClearPoint is embedded in drug delivery and laser therapy workflows that larger companies are building around.
