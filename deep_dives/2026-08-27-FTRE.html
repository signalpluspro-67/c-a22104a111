Introducing FTRE

Fortrea Holdings is a contract research organization, a CRO. It runs clinical trials for pharmaceutical, biotech, and medical device customers, from early phase I studies through phase IV and real-world evidence work.

The company also has a consulting arm covering product development, regulatory strategy, market access, and health economics. It was incorporated in 2023 after a separation from its former parent, and it's headquartered in Durham, North Carolina. Roughly 14,000 people work there.

The edge is straightforward. Drug companies need trials run on time and on protocol. They'd rather pay a specialist than build that capability in-house for every program. Fortrea competes on execution, global site networks, and data management. The industry isn't new, and neither is the business model, but the demand stays steady because clinical trials don't stop when the macro economy slows.
