Introducing PCG

PG&E Corporation owns Pacific Gas and Electric, the regulated utility serving northern and central California. It delivers electricity and natural gas to residential, commercial, industrial, and agricultural customers. The generation mix spans nuclear, hydroelectric, fossil fuels, fuel cells, and solar. The company owns transmission lines, substations, distribution lines, and gas storage. It emerged from bankruptcy in 2020 after wildfire liabilities, and the post-reorganization entity carries a cleaner cost structure and a state-backed framework for future fire costs.

The stock is a slow-moving regulated franchise. Revenue is flat. Earnings are recovering. The balance sheet still carries heavy debt, but the earnings multiple is compressed. This is a company whose equity story is about normalization, not growth. That makes a short-dated call unusual. The desk is not buying the stock's five-year story. It is renting a short window for a break above a round number.
