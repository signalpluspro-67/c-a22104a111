Introducing PGY

Pagaya Technologies builds AI-driven credit underwriting infrastructure for lenders. The company sits between banks, fintechs, and institutional investors. Its partners send loan applications that they would otherwise decline. Pagaya's models evaluate those applications in real time and route the approved ones to its funding network.

The core products are Decline Monetization, Dual Look, and First Look. Decline Monetization takes rejected applications and finds borrowers the partner would have turned away. Dual Look runs Pagaya's models concurrently with the partner's own underwriting. First Look routes specific application segments to Pagaya's network before the partner's standard process kicks in.

The customer base includes fintech lenders, incumbent banks, auto finance providers, and residential real estate services firms. Pagaya was founded in 2016 and is headquartered in New York. The company has 511 employees. It operates in the United States, Israel, and the Cayman Islands.
