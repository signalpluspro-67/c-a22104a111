Introducing ABCL

AbCellera Biologics Inc. is a Canadian antibody discovery and development company headquartered in Vancouver. The core business is its platform: a full stack of technologies that find, engineer, and develop antibody medicines, then license or partner them to larger pharmaceutical companies. It has collaborations with Eli Lilly, AbbVie, Jazz Pharmaceuticals, Biogen, and Vertex Pharmaceuticals, among others.

The company runs its own pipeline too. ABCL635 is a non-hormonal medicine in Phase 2 for moderate to severe vasomotor symptoms, which is the clinical term for hot flashes and related menopause symptoms. ABCL575 is a half-life extended monoclonal antibody in Phase 1 for T-cell mediated autoimmune conditions like atopic dermatitis.

That current ratio of 12.77 means the company can operate for years without needing to dilute. The edge is simple: a platform that gets paid to make antibodies, and a pipeline that lets it keep the upside when those antibodies work.
