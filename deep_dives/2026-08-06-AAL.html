Introducing AAL

American Airlines Group operates as a network air carrier across the United States, Latin America, the Atlantic, and the Pacific. The company runs scheduled air transportation for passengers and cargo through major hubs including Charlotte, Chicago, Dallas/Fort Worth, Los Angeles, Miami, New York, Philadelphia, Phoenix, and Washington, D.C. It also operates through partner gateways in London, Doha, Madrid, Seattle/Tacoma, Sydney, and Tokyo.

The mainline fleet totals 1,013 aircraft. The company traces its roots back to 1926 and restructured from AMR Corporation to American Airlines Group in December 2013.

American's edge is scale. It is one of the largest network carriers in the world, with a hub-and-spoke system that gives it density advantages on the most profitable routes. The loyalty program, the corporate contracts, and the slot portfolio at constrained airports all create a moat that low-cost carriers cannot replicate at the same margins.
