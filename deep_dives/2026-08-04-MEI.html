Introducing MEI

Methode Electronics designs and manufactures mechatronic products across four segments: Automotive, Industrial, Interface, and Medical. The company was incorporated in 1946 and is headquartered in Southfield, Michigan.

The Automotive segment is the largest revenue driver. Methode supplies power distribution solutions including busbars, smart connect systems, battery disconnect units, and integrated circuit boards to OEMs and tiered suppliers. It also provides specialized LED lighting and advanced sensor applications. This is the EV exposure. Every electric vehicle needs power distribution architecture, and Methode's busbars and disconnect units are specified into those platforms.

The Industrial segment is the data-center and infrastructure play. Methode manufactures high-current, high-voltage flexible power cabling systems, laminated busbars, and custom power-product assemblies used in data centers, aerospace, commercial vehicles, telecommunications, and power conversion. The Interface segment adds high-speed copper communication solutions for data networking and broadband. You are buying a company that touches both vehicle electrification and cloud infrastructure through distinct product lines.
