Introducing AXON

Axon Enterprise builds the technology stack for public safety. The company runs two segments. Software and Services includes Axon Evidence, Axon Records, Draft One, Axon Fusus, and Axon Assistant. Connected Devices covers TASER conducted energy weapons, body cameras, in-car cameras, drones, counter-drone tools, and VR training hardware.

You know the TASER brand. The company was called TASER International until April 2017, then changed its name to Axon Enterprise. It is headquartered in Scottsdale, Arizona, and employs about 5100 people. The customer base is first responders across federal, state, and local agencies, plus international government buyers, enterprises, and consumers through direct sales and distributors.

The edge is the network effect. Hardware captures evidence, software stores and analyzes it, and the subscription layer keeps agencies locked in. Axon Evidence and Axon Records create switching costs that a pure hardware vendor cannot match. That is why the market has historically paid a premium for this name, sometimes an extreme one.
