Introducing TCBK

The company has been around since 1975. No fintech overlay, no crypto exposure, no exotic lending. This is a deposit-gathering, loan-making operation in Northern California.

That simplicity matters. In a sector where credit risk is the primary fear, a plain-vanilla regional bank with a 1.4 price-to-book ratio and a 13.7 trailing P/E does not demand heroic assumptions. You are not pricing in a transformation. You are pricing in a return to recent trading levels.

Risk Factors
