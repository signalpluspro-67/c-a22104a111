Introducing FSTR

L.B. Foster Company has been building and supplying infrastructure since 1902. It operates two segments: Rail, Technologies, and Services, and Infrastructure Solutions.

The Rail segment is the heritage business. Foster sells new and used rail to passenger and short line freight railroads, industrial companies, and rail contractors. It supplies track spikes, bolts, angle bars, tie plates, insulated rail joints, fixation fasteners, coverboards, power rail, and trackwork products. It also builds friction management systems, wheel impact load detection equipment, wayside data collection systems, and monitoring for rockfall, flood, earthworks, and bridge strikes. Aftermarket services round out the segment.

The Infrastructure Solutions segment manufactures precast concrete products under the CXT brand. That includes restrooms, concession stands, and protective storage buildings for national, state, and municipal parks. It also produces sound walls, bridge beams, and wet and dry utility concrete products. The segment supplies steel bridge products, corrosion protection solutions, pipe threading and painting for oil and gas production, fabricated steel and aluminum, and protective pipeline coating services. The company serves the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and international markets.
