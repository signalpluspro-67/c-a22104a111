Introducing GENI

Genius Sports Limited provides technology products and services to the sports, sports betting, and sports media industries. The company operates in the Americas, Europe, and internationally. It is headquartered in London and was founded in 2001.

The company collects and distributes live optical tracking, event data, and video for leagues. It offers streaming solutions that let leagues commercialize their video footage. It provides integrity services to monitor betting markets and flag suspicious activity. It also builds fan engagement tools, digital content, and gamification solutions.

Its two standout products are BetVision and FanHub. BetVision is an interactive single-screen live streaming solution with integrated bet slips, real-time statistics, and personalized viewing modes. FanHub is an advertising solution for creating, delivering, and optimizing digital marketing campaigns. These are high-margin software-like offerings layered on top of the data pipes.
