Introducing AVR

Anteris Technologies Global Corp. is an Australian structural heart company. The core product is the DurAVR transcatheter heart valve system, designed for the treatment of severe aortic stenosis. The valve is shaped to mimic the performance of a healthy human aortic valve, which is the clinical differentiator. Standard valves are round. The native aortic valve is not. Anteris built a valve that matches the anatomy.

The company also develops ADAPT anti-calcification tissue, which transforms xenograft tissue into durable bioscaffolds that resist the calcium buildup that destroys conventional bioprosthetic valves over time. The ComASUR delivery system is the physician-developed catheter that places the valve. It is steerable and reinforced to navigate heart anatomy without damaging the aorta. The full platform is valve, tissue, and delivery.

Anteris employs 174 people. It is a small-cap medical device company with $770M in market capitalization and a product platform targeting a market worth billions. The clinical promise is real. The commercial revenue is almost nonexistent. You are buying the inflection, the platform, and the insider conviction, the profits.
