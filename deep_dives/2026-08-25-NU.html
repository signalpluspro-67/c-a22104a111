Introducing Nu Holdings Ltd.

Nu Holdings is a digital banking platform born in São Paulo. The company does not operate physical branches. It delivers credit cards, personal accounts, business accounts, loans, insurance, investments, and crypto trading through a mobile app. You can think of it as a super-app for financial services across Latin America. Brazil is its home market, with growing operations in Mexico and Colombia. The product set covers spending, saving, borrowing, investing, and protecting assets.

The core edge is cost structure. A digital-native architecture keeps customer acquisition and servicing costs low. That allows Nu to extend credit to segments that legacy banks underserve. The thesis is straightforward: as more customers join and deepen their engagement, revenue scales faster than costs. High margins and rapid growth confirm it is working. The stock is now drawing attention for a near-term trade opportunity alongside its long-term story.
