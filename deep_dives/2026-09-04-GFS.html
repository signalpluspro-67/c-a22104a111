Introducing GFS

GLOBALFOUNDRIES is a semiconductor foundry based in Malta, New York. It makes chips for other companies. Microprocessors, radio frequency modems, power management units, microcontrollers. It is a mainstream wafer fabrication business with roughly 14,000 employees across the United States, Europe, the Middle East, and Africa.

The company has a strategic partnership with the U.S. Department of Energy's Genesis Mission to accelerate scientific discovery through AI and advanced computing. That is a real foothold in the government-backed compute buildout. It also provides quantum technology solutions.

GFS is not a cutting-edge node leader like TSMC. It plays in the mainstream and specialty node space. That is a lower-cost, lower-margin part of the foundry market, but it is also the segment that benefits when customers want a second source outside of Taiwan.
