The Big Picture

Introducing GSHD

Goosehead Insurance, Inc. is a holding company that runs a personal lines insurance agency platform across the United States. It distributes homeowner's, auto, dwelling, flood, wind, earthquake, excess liability, and small business policies. It also offers life insurance and coverage for motorcycles and recreational vehicles.
The company uses two channels to reach clients. Corporate locations sell directly. Franchise locations are owned and operated by independent agents who license the Goosehead brand, technology, and carrier access. This franchise structure keeps capital costs low while the company earns royalty fees and benefits from the aggregate premium volume when negotiating with carriers.
Goosehead was founded in 2003 and is headquartered in Westlake, Texas. It employs 1600 people. The stock is small and it moves. That's the point.
