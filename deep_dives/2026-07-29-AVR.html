Introducing AVR

Anteris Technologies Global Corp. is a structural heart company based in Toowong, Australia. It develops and commercializes minimally invasive medical devices to treat heart valve diseases. The core product is the DurAVR transcatheter heart valve system, a novel transcatheter aortic valve designed to mimic the performance of a healthy human aortic valve for patients with severe aortic stenosis.

The company also develops ADAPT anti-calcification tissue technology. This transforms xenograft tissue into durable bioscaffolds that mimic human tissue for surgical repair. The ComASUR delivery system is a physician-developed balloon expandable delivery system with a reinforced steerable catheter, designed for precise deflection through heart anatomy without damaging the aorta.

The bet here is on clinical and regulatory milestones converting into commercial revenue, not on current sales.
