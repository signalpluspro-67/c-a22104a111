Introducing AMN

AMN Healthcare Services is a healthcare workforce company based in Dallas, Texas, founded in 1985. It staffs hospitals and care facilities across the United States with travel nurses, allied health professionals, physicians, and interim leaders. The company operates through three segments: Nurse and Allied Solutions, Physician and Leadership Solutions, and Technology and Workforce Solutions.

The core business is travel nurse staffing. That segment fills critical gaps at acute care hospitals when permanent staff are short or when labor disruptions hit. AMN also provides locum tenens physician staffing, executive search for healthcare leaders, and language services. Its Technology and Workforce Solutions segment sells vendor management systems and outsourced staffing solutions to hospitals that want to consolidate their contingent labor procurement.

The edge here is scale and integrated offering. Smith. Hospitals come to AMN because it can fill a travel nurse contract, a locum tenens physician slot, and a vendor management software need under one roof. That breadth creates switching costs and gives AMN pricing power during staffing crises, when hospitals are most desperate and least price-sensitive.
