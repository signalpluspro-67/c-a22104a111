Introducing GS

Goldman Sachs is the premier global investment bank. It operates through three segments: Global Banking and Markets, Asset and Wealth Management, and Platform Solutions. The Global Banking and Markets segment is the engine. It covers M&A advisory, equity and debt underwriting, lending, and client execution across cash and derivative instruments. When companies raise capital or do deals, Goldman is at the table.

Asset and Wealth Management manages money across equity, fixed income, hedge funds, private equity, real estate, and other strategies. This segment gives Goldman fee revenue that compounds with assets under management. Platform Solutions handles transaction banking, deposit-taking, payment solutions, and cash management for corporate and institutional clients.

The company was founded in 1869 and is headquartered in New York. Its edge is franchise strength. Goldman commands the top tier of advisory mandates and capital markets assignments. When the cycle turns favorable for dealmaking and capital raising, Goldman captures a disproportionate share of the revenue.
