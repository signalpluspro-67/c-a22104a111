Introducing AMTB

Amerant Bancorp Inc. is a Florida bank holding company based in Coral Gables. It operates Amerant Bank, a regional institution serving individuals and businesses across the United States. The company was founded in 1979 and renamed from Mercantil Bank Holding Corporation in June 2019.

The bank offers checking, savings, business, and money market accounts, cash management, certificates of deposit, and a full lending book. Loans span commercial real estate, owner-occupied properties, single-family residential, commercial, financial institution loans, and consumer lending including autos, personal lines, credit cards, and securities-secured loans. It also runs a meaningful wealth management and fiduciary business for high net worth clients, plus retail banking, mortgage, brokerage, and investment advisory services in global capital markets.

The edge is focus. It is small, liquid enough for a patient position, and trades at $28.84. The bank blends traditional Florida community banking with private-client wealth services. That dual revenue stream gives it a little more resilience than a single-line community lender.
