The Big Picture

Technology is in a momentum regime and still riding that acceleration. The sector has been leading the broad market, and the data behind it supports continued rotation into software infrastructure.

Cloud spending has shifted from a question of whether companies adopt to how fast they scale. Enterprises run their applications across AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud simultaneously. Founder-led companies bring a structural advantage here. A founder CEO with deep technical conviction allocates capital toward the right product bets years before a hired operator would. Datadog fits that profile. The macro backdrop rewards a company selling mission-critical visibility into cloud environments while the sector itself carries momentum.

Introducing DDOG

Datadog sells a single platform that monitors cloud applications end to end. You get infrastructure metrics, application performance, log management, and security in one place. The product portfolio spans real user monitoring, database monitoring, network monitoring, cloud SIEM, and newer entries like LLM observability for AI workloads.

The edge is integration. Customers buy one dashboard instead of stitching together five vendors. Datadog expanded from pure infrastructure monitoring into application performance, logs, and security because the data pipeline underneath is shared. Every new product lands on the same backend. Cross-sell happens naturally.

The company serves over 8,100 employees and operates globally from its New York headquarters. Its platform targets the operations and engineering teams who need to keep cloud applications running. When a service degrades, Datadog is the tool those teams open first.
