Introducing MEI

Methode Electronics is a 1946 company headquartered in Southfield, Michigan. It designs, engineers, and manufactures mechatronic products across four segments: Automotive, Industrial, Interface, and Medical.

The Automotive segment is the largest revenue driver. It supplies power distribution solutions, battery disconnect units, smart connect systems, integrated circuit boards, LED lighting, and advanced sensor applications to OEMs and their tiered suppliers. This is the electrification play inside the company.

The Industrial segment builds exterior and interior lighting, industrial safety radio remote controls, braided flexible cables, laminated busbars, and high-current power cabling systems. These serve aerospace, commercial vehicles, data centers, military, power conversion, and telecommunications. The Interface segment provides high-speed digital communication over copper media for data networking and broadband, plus user interface panel solutions for appliances. The company serves transportation, cloud computing, construction equipment, and consumer appliance end markets.

It is a small-cap component supplier with exposure to the right secular trends, trading at a valuation that reflects years of poor execution. The stock is telling you the execution is turning.
