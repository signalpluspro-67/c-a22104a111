Introducing ETSY

Etsy operates two-sided online marketplaces. Buyers show up for unique or secondhand goods. Sellers pay listing fees, transaction fees, payments processing fees, and optional advertising and shipping label fees. The company doesn't hold inventory. It collects on every side of the transaction.

The main marketplace connects artisans and vintage sellers with consumers. Depop is a fashion resale marketplace with a younger demographic. Both businesses share the same engine: network effects from concentrated seller communities and high gross margins. Etsy's gross margin is 71.4%, which tells you the core marketplace economics are strong before you get to the operating line.

The balance sheet is the ugly part. That's a company that has returned capital aggressively or acquired assets at premiums. The asset-light model generates cash, but the capital structure is not a fortress. You're trading the operational story, not the liquidation value.
