Introducing WDAY

Workday, Inc. builds enterprise cloud applications for finance and human resources. Its platform spans financial management, spend management, expense management, human capital management, planning, and supply chain solutions for healthcare and education.

The core edge is the unified data model. Every module, from payroll to financial consolidation, writes to the same underlying object structure. That is why companies adopt Workday for HR and then expand into financials. The switching cost is enormous once the system of record is embedded.

Workday serves professional services, financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, government, education, retail, and technology. Sells through direct sales. The company went public with a reputation for high-touch enterprise deployments, and that model shows up in a 75.8% gross margin.
